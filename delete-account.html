Delete your StoryGram account

You can delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open StoryGram and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you're signed out on every device.

Without the app

Email storygram.support@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my StoryGram account” and your username. We confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Email address, username, display name, profile picture, bio and creator profile
  • Password / Google sign-in link, sessions, trusted devices, push-notification token
  • Reading progress, library, bookmarks and reading history linked to you

What is kept

  • Stories, chapters, comments and posts you published stay visible to other readers, shown as “Deleted user” and no longer linked to any personal data. To have them removed as well, ask us by email.
  • Database backups containing old data are overwritten within 30 days; server logs within 30 days.
