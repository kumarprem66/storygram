Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · App: StoryGram (Android, package com.premkumartechnology633.storygram)

StoryGram ("we", "us") is a platform for reading and publishing stories. This policy explains what personal data the app and its server collect, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Contact: storygram.support@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data — email address, username, display name, and either a password (stored only as a one-way hash) or your Google sign-in. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile data you choose to add — profile picture, bio, pen name, creator profile details, language and genre preferences.
  • Content you create — stories, chapters, covers, comments, reviews, ratings, reactions, social posts, follows, reports you file.
  • Reading activity — reading progress, bookmarks, library and favourites, reading time and streaks, so the app can resume where you left off and show your history (public only if you turn that on in Settings).
  • Device and technical data — a push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and for security your sign-in sessions with IP address, device/app type and time. Our server keeps standard request logs (time, address requested, status) for troubleshooting.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos you don't upload, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To provide the service: show and publish stories, sync reading progress, recommendations, search, notifications you've enabled.
  • To send service emails (for example account verification).
  • To keep the community safe: handle reports, enforce our Terms, prevent abuse (rate limiting, fraud prevention).

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Other users see what you publish and your public profile (username, display name, avatar, bio, creator profile, published content, comments).
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf: Oracle Cloud (server and database hosting, India), Google Firebase (sign-in and push notifications), Google (email delivery).
  • Legal reasons — when required by law or to protect users and the service.

4. Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to the server and database is restricted.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Delete account) or by following these steps. Deleting removes your email, username, profile, sign-in data, sessions and reading data linked to you; content you published (stories, comments, posts) may remain visible but is shown as "Deleted user" and is no longer linked to you. Backups are overwritten within 30 days. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

6. Your rights

You can access and correct your profile in the app, choose whether your reading activity is public, turn notifications off, and delete your account. To request a copy of your data or ask a privacy question, email storygram.support@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

7. Children

StoryGram is not intended for children. You must be 18 or older, or use the app with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal data without that consent, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
