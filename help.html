Help & support

Contact us

Email storygram.support@gmail.com — we usually reply within 2 working days. Please include your username and, for a problem with a story or comment, its title.

Common questions

I didn't get the verification email. Check your spam folder, then use “Resend” on the sign-in screen.

How do I publish a story? Open your profile, switch to Creator, create a story, add chapters, then publish.

How do I report something? Use the Report option on the comment or feed post. For a story or a profile, email us with its title or username.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Settings → Delete account. Without the app: see Delete your account.
